    Put it on your home screen
    iPhone

    Open this page in Safari. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.

    Android

    Open this page in Chrome. Tap Install app when it appears, or the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen.

    It opens full screen with the Arc & Iron badge and works offline once it has loaded.

    Back up your data

    Your log, check-ins, skills and program progress are saved on this device only. Download a backup now and then, and restore it on a new phone or after clearing your browser.

    Train in clear space with room overhead and behind you. Swinging moves like the 360 punish sloppy shoulders, so learn them with your lightest mace or club and stop a set when form fades.

    Not medical advice. Arc & Iron is a training aid. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise program, especially with an injury or health condition, and stop any movement that causes pain.

    Volume

    Also turn up your phone's volume. On iPhone, check the silent switch.

    This phone did not let the app keep the screen on. To stop it locking mid-workout, set Auto-Lock to Never for now (iPhone: Settings › Display & Brightness › Auto-Lock; Android: Settings › Display › Screen timeout), and set it back afterward.
