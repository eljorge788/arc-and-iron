Flow library

A flow chains three or more moves so each one starts where the last one ended. Learn each piece slowly, then link them and breathe with the movement. The builder draws from these.

Train in clear space with room overhead and behind you. Swinging moves like the 360 punish sloppy shoulders, so learn them with your lightest mace or club and stop a set when form fades. This is a training aid, not medical advice.
