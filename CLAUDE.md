# Arc & Iron

Chris's kettlebell, steel mace and steel club workout app, published with GitHub Pages at
https://eljorge788.github.io/arc-and-iron/

## Layout
- `index.html`: the whole app (markup, CSS and JS in one file, no build step).
- `sw.js`: service worker. Network-first for the page so updates reach phones on next open. Bump `CACHE` when changing icons or the manifest.
- `demos.js`: animated move demos (front and side views) for the mace, the steel club and the kettlebell. A pose's `len` picks the implement (MACE or CLUB); `kb:1` draws a kettlebell instead (G = handle middle, D = handle to bell, W = along the handle, hands at `hR`/`hL` along W and `hD` along D). Club moves follow the beginner reference in the Arc & Iron project (steel-club-exercises.md), and the side swing (two-hand and single-arm) follows steel-club-side-swing.md; kettlebell moves follow Chris's lesson notes from Mark Wildman's kettlebell lessons 1-16 and his "3 Types of Goblet Squats" (also in the project). Two clubs: a pose with `G2`/`D2` puts a second club in the left hand (on its pommel); `dblClub()` mirrors a one-hand club pose into both hands. Swings and swipes keep the arms straight on the way down: a pose with `lock` ('R', 'L', 'B' two hands, '2' two clubs) has the swinging arm straight, and between two locked poses the hand travels on an arc around the shoulder (`armArc()`); `lockSwing(kf,lock)` straightens and locks every pose of a move where the hand hangs well below the shoulder. Two kettlebells: `G2`/`D2`/`W2` put a second bell in the left hand; `dblBell()` mirrors a one-hand kettlebell pose into both hands. `carry()` moves a hold written for standing tall onto a squatting or leaning body. A pose's `twist` turns the chest about the spine (after pitch, yaw and roll; the windmill uses it to stack the shoulders). Mace holds follow Chris's definitions (project doc mace-holds.md): `STAND` is the order position (lead hand on top, hands at the belly button, mace straight up), the offset hold puts the lead hand along the shaft, choking up moves the hands toward the head, the row position (`ROW_L()`/`ROW_R()`) is a split hold with the mace hanging level at the lead side on long straight arms (a dead hang), head behind (the uppercut and paddle start there, and `swordSwitch(from)` hands it from one side's row position to the other's), and high ready (seatbelt, `HIGH_READY()`, demo `mace:high ready`) holds the mace on a diagonal across the body, lead hand at the head by the lead shoulder, base at the other hip. One-hand kettlebell positions (rack, lockout, swing and clean) are built for the right hand; `mirror()` gives the left.
- Flow demos: `Demos.openFlow()` chains the step demos. A flow listed in `FLOW_TL` (demos.js) is instead built as one continuous movement by `flowTL()`: moves run into each other with no stop at order, the cycle repeats mirrored for the other lead side, and each mark carries its step index and side for highlighting. Kettlebell flows use `flowTLs()` (parts carry their own side, or none for two-hand flows such as the Halo Flow) and `kbSidedFlow()` (right-hand parts, then mirrored for the left, with a floor-to-hike intro and a park-the-bell outro); the hand change is a hand-to-hand swing switch. Mace flows use `maceFlow(parts,names,first,steps2)`: parts are written for one lead (top) hand and mirrored; a switch step (or a part flagged true, such as the high grab into the row position) changes the lead so the labels follow it; `prayerOrder()` is the prayer switch between moves (order to a horizontal hold with the lead hand at the head, prayer at the center, horizontal on the other side, then on into the next move), `highGrabToRow()` the support hand's high overhand grab into the row position, `uppercutRep()`/`uppercutToFront()` one uppercut back to the row or down in front into order.
- `art.js`: theme-aware SVG artwork (tab backgrounds, program and skill-path heroes). Brief in `art.brief.md`. `S.art` picks poster, engraved or off.
- `keepawake.js`: keeps the screen on during guided workouts (Wake Lock API, with the NoSleep.js video fallback, MIT).
- `manifest.webmanifest` and `icons/`: home-screen install. `icons/badge.svg` is the master logo.

## Data
User data lives in each browser's localStorage (`ironflow`, `ironflow-logs`, `ironflow-checks`, `ironflow-state`, `ironflow-setup`, `ironflow-resume`). Keep these key names and record shapes backward compatible so updates never wipe anyone's history. Saved state must never be written before it is read: `Store.saveState()`/`saveLocal()` do nothing until `Store.loadLocal()` has run (`Store.loaded`). Startup renders settings before loading, so anything called from `renderSettings()` or other startup renders must not depend on `Store.state` being real. Version d39afe0 broke this (an early save from `notePathLevels()` wiped progress on every open); `recoverFromLogs()` runs once after load (flag `Store.state.recovered`) and rebuilds programs, path steps and badges from the log, adding only. Test any startup change by loading a saved state in jsdom and checking it survives. When a move or flow is renamed, add the old name to `RENAMED` (old log name to new) so its history, last weight and progression carry over; logs themselves are never rewritten.
Equipment: `S.setups` holds named setups and `S.eq` always points at the active setup's equipment (older saves without setups are migrated into a "Home" setup by `linkSetups()`). Rack weights are unit-tagged keys (`"16kg"`, `"35lb"`) so one implement can mix kg and lb makers: `wP()` parses a key, weight string or log entry into `{v,u,kg,key}`, `sortRack()` keeps `st.w` in real-weight order (and migrates older numeric weights using `st.unit`), and `rackList()` returns the parsed items. `st.unit` is now only the unit weights are shown in; `wTxt(x,eq)` converts for display and, if `S.showListed` (default on), adds the listed weight after a converted one. Workout weights (`o.wt`) and logs keep the listed weight and its unit; progression, records and the lift table compare real weight (`wKg()`/`mKg()`), and `up.from` / `need.cur` are now strings with a unit (older numeric ones still read). `S.today` is a today-only tweak; the generator reads equipment through `effEq()`. The in-app backup file uses `app: "arc-and-iron"`.
In progress: `Store.state.week` is the saved weekly plan: `plan` is an array of `{days}` (objects, not nested arrays, so it syncs), one per week; `phases` names each week (learn, build, push, light; `S.planWeeks` 1/4/6 picks the length); `done` and `skip` map `"week:Day"` to time; each day's `weekRef` is `{id,day,wk}`. Week 1 is built up front; later days are stubs that `ensureBuilt()` builds when first opened (Build/Push weeks reuse the week-1 moves via `refreshWorkout()`, which reworks weights and targets from the latest logs; the Lighter week is a shorter fresh build with `opts.light`). `planNorm()` migrates older one-week saves (`days`, `done` keyed by day) and must be used instead of reading `Store.state.week` directly. Startup shows a saved plan and never rebuilds over it; Build plan asks before replacing one in progress. A plan can be ended (`endPlan()`, two taps, from the plan view on Build or the Continue card) or replaced (`newPlan()` switches the builder to Weekly plan and scrolls to it); both, and any rebuild over a plan, record it in `Store.state.plansEnded` via `archivePlan()`. Ending clears `Store.state.week`; logs, `plansDone` and achievements stay. `Store.state.saved` (via `savedList()`) keeps every single workout built and not yet logged (newest first, up to 8, 21 days), so a new build never overwrites another; `current.sid` is the saved entry on screen and `keepBuilt()` replaces it in place. `Store.state.lastBuilt` mirrors the newest for older code. The Build tab shows chips for the plan and each saved workout (`buildBarHTML()`). `ironflow-resume` holds an unfinished guided workout.
Programs: `Store.state.programs` (via `progList()`) lists every running program `{id,start,done,days}`; `days` are the chosen weekdays for Day 1, Day 2 ... (`suggestDays()` picks free, spaced days; `schedPickHTML()` is the picker). Older saves with one `Store.state.program` are moved into the list. When a program's last session is done, `progList()` moves it out into `Store.state.progDone` (`progDoneList()`, newest first, `{id,start,end,done,days}`, via `closeProg()`, which also drops its leftover saved workouts), so it leaves the schedule and frees its weekdays; the Programs tab lists it under Completed with Run it again, Continue shows a Program complete card for 7 days, and its achievement reads `progDone`. `progList()` and `savedList()` edit their arrays in place: never hold one across a call that might reassign it.
Schedule: `upcoming(n)` places every unfinished plan and program session on its next matching weekday from today. The log calendar (`renderCal()`), Continue's "Scheduled today", the program picker and extra workouts read it. Single builds and the recommendation pass `opts.around` from `schedAround()`, which avoids the main muscle groups of today's unfinished and tomorrow's sessions and follows the plan's lighter week.
Weights: `wtAdj()`/`stepWt()` let you step any move's weight through your rack before starting (`orig` keeps the built weight); the log form steps through the rack too and shows the planned weight. Hands: `HAND_SETS` groups moves that are the same exercise one-handed, two-handed or with two implements (two bells / two clubs); `handSeg()` draws the picker and `switchHands()` swaps in the real move from X (new cue, demo, history and per-side dose; a weight changed by hand carries over). The picker sits beside the weight on the workout sheet, in guided mode (current move, list workouts and the Get ready screen, with − / + via `gMoveCtl()`; `gRegen()` rebuilds the steps and keeps your place) and on the log form (`data-lh`, which also sets `dbl`). Two-implement options need matching pairs in the setup, except on the log. Guided steps carry `secI/secT` (working sets in the section) and `mvI/mvT` (per move) for the sets done/left line (`gSetsHTML()`). Logging a workout calls `finishedWorkout()` to tick these off. The Continue tab reads all of them.
Goal and achievements: `Store.state.goal` is the weekly goal (`hist` keeps the goal in force each week, so changing it never rewrites past weeks). The streak is not stored: `goalStats()` recounts it from the log against the goal. A first goal applies back to the first logged week (`setGoal()`), and after the d39afe0 wipe (`Store.state.recovered`) the empty goal card explains the loss, suggests a target from recent weeks (`goalGuess()`) and re-credits goal badges quietly (`achQuiet`). `Store.state.ach` maps achievement id to the time earned and is never recomputed away; the first run credits past history silently. `Store.state.plansDone` lists weekly plans finished in full. Achievements are defined in `ACH` (groups in `ACH_GROUPS`); records come from `achCtx()` / `recordsIn()`. Log records may carry `kinds` (workout styles), `counts` (guided AMRAP rounds, ladder rungs, deck cards, with `kind` and `min`), `guided` and `restFlow`; older logs simply lack them.
Coach (automatic progression, on unless `S.prog===false`): `progPick()` starts each move from its last logged weight and steps up one rack weight after two good sessions (per-move `feel` e/r/h on log moves, else session effort ≤7), holds on Hard, and drops one step in a lighter week (`Store.state.coach.deload` = that Monday). `coachBlock()` sets AMRAP/deck targets, EMOM reps (log field `emom`) and ladder height (count field `top`). Moves/flows that changed carry `up:{from,dir}`; `undoProg()` reverts a workout and sets `opts.noProg`. `coachChecks()` (on every renderTrack) starts lighter weeks, changes `S.level` (never within 21 days of `S.levelAt`) and marks skill steps, each leaving a note in `Store.state.coach.notes` with Undo. A note's `id` is always its own (`coachNote()` sets it last); a skill note keeps its step id in `sid` (step ids contain ':' and once broke the buttons; `fixNotes()` repairs older saves), and notes with nothing to say are retired quietly. Two more note kinds: `plevel` (from `notePathLevels()`, run by `syncPathLevel()`: an implement's path raised its workout level; `coach.pwl` remembers the last level per implement, first seen silently; Undo sets `S.lvlHold[eq]`, which `pathLvlFor()` applies until that path moves up again) and `gear` (from `noteGear()`, which wraps every `buildWorkout()`: a move is ready for heavier and nothing heavier is owned; once per implement, size and pair via `coach.gearSeen`; its second button adds the size to the setup, and the note retires itself once that weight is owned). When a move is ready to go up but the rack has nothing heavier, `progPick()` returns `need:{next,cur,unit,dbl}` (next standard size from `EQ[eq].lists`); `gearHTML()` suggests it with an add-to-setup button. Guided mode inserts a `prep` step ("Get ready", `S.prep` seconds, default 30, 0 = off; cool-down capped at 10) before every section after the first, never inside one. The recommended workout is `recommend()`, cached per day in `Store.state.rec` by `recSig()`.
Skill paths: `PATH_DEF` lists each implement's Basics (pick up and set down, holds and positions; `k` ids) and then the Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced steps in learning order, one step per move or flow (by exact X/FLOWS name). `PATHS[eq].steps` is built from it with permanent ids (`eq:basic:k`, `eq:<slug of name>`) and `lvl` 0-3; goals come from `PATH_GOALS` or `stepGoal()`. A flow sits at its own level and any higher-level move it needs is placed just before it (kb Alternating halo and Thrusters, club H2H clean in Beginner; kb Windmill in Intermediate). Every X move and FLOWS flow must appear exactly once: add new moves to `PATH_DEF` when adding them. Progress is `Store.state.path` {stepId: time} (`setStep()`); the old numbered `Store.state.skills` is kept only for the legacy path badges (shown only if earned). `pOpen()` is the next `PATH_WIN` (3) steps in order and `pOpenAll()` adds the next 3 moves/flows; `learnSet()` (mastered + next 3 moves/flows) is what `pool()` and `flowBlock()` build from when `S.useSkills` is on (widening the window if too few), and `syncPathLevel()` then sets `S.level` from the paths (lowest across active gear; the effort-based level change is skipped). `S.pathLock` (default on): `needBasics()` blocks weekly plans for gear without Basics (plans leave that gear out) and blocks starting programs (`progNeedBasics()`); running ones are not affected. Achievements: group `paths`, one per level per implement (`pl-eq-l`), tier = level, with `TIERS[0]` Stone below Iron. Coach auto-masters an open move/flow step after three solid logs; Basics are marked by hand.
Practice: `buildPractice(eq)` makes a guided session from `pOpenAll(eq)` (up to 6 steps, Basics included, lightest weight): a block of kind `Practice` (moves carry `pid`, `basic`, `fl` for flows, `demo`) that `practiceGuided()` turns into two rounds per step (flows timed 2 min, others tap-done) with 45 s rests. The workout carries `label` (shown instead of the goal name) and `practice:{eq,ids}`; `prefillLog()` passes it on, the log form asks Solid / Not yet per step (`lgPrac`), the log record gets `practice:{eq,solid}`, and `applyPractice()` keeps the dates each step was rated solid in `Store.state.practice` and masters a step solid on two different days. Started from the path card (`data-pp`), Continue (`pract:eq`) and the walkthrough.
Movement limits: `S.limits` holds `LIMITS` keys (overhead, knee, ballistic, twist, hinge, floor). `moveTags()` tags library moves (overhead from `OH_MOVES`, floor from `FLOOR_MOVES`, the rest from pattern, flags and name), `looseTags()` matches flow steps and warm-up drills to library moves, and `flowTags()` unions a flow's steps. `pool()`, `forced()`, `flowBlock()` and `warmBlock()` skip anything limited; path steps that are limited (`limitedStep()`) are set aside: skipped by `pOpen`/`pOpenAll`/`learnSet` and not counted toward finishing a level (`lvlReq()`), badges included. Programs warn when sessions include limited parts (`progLimHTML()`). New moves going overhead or to the floor need adding to `OH_MOVES` / `FLOOR_MOVES`.
First run: `FIRST_RUN` (nothing saved when the app opened) and no logs opens the walkthrough (`openOnboard()`, element `#onb`): gear and weights, focus, Basics already known (marks them done), movement limits, then a first practice or workout. `ironflow-onboard` = '1' once done or skipped; Session settings has "Run the setup walkthrough again".
Lead side: every club and mace move (and every one-sided move, flag `u`) has a lead side; `sided()` decides. The lead side is the top hand, for both club and mace; a two-hand club is held on the top hand's side of the body (in demos, `topSwap()` fixes a move built with the club on the other side). On one-handed moves it is the working hand. Order positions favour the lead side (two-hand `C_ORDER2` sits toward the top hand; one-hand `C_ORDER1` is at the working shoulder). Workouts call out the switch: halfway through each interval (guided mode says "Switch lead side" at the halfway mark via `step.half`), reps split per side in EMOM, AMRAP, ladder, deck and strength, each time the move comes round in Tabata, and halfway through club and mace warm-up drills. Every club and mace flow has a hand switch step (or ends on a switch move, such as the switch shield cast, that hands the club over); `sw:'mid'` flows cover both lead sides in one cycle, `sw:'end'` flows switch at the end so cycles alternate. Keep `u` for one-handed moves; don't mark two-hand club or mace moves `u` to get side handling. Switch moves (flag `a`, hands change every rep) have the switch built in: `sided()` is false for them and their doses say "alternating hands".
Hands: club moves are two-handed unless their flags say `1` (one hand) or `2` (two clubs); `handsOf()` gives the label ("One hand", "Two hands", "Two clubs") shown on workout cards, guided lists and guided steps. A club move that can be done either way is two separate entries (e.g. Torch press and Two-hand torch press) so a workout always names the version; the demo pop-up can still switch between them via `alt`.
Build tab layout: every section (coach notes, recommended, equipment, build a workout, your workout) is a `details.bsec`, closed when the app opens, with a one-line status label kept current by `bsSync()` (run from a MutationObserver on `#v-build`; empty sections are hidden). Anything that puts a workout on screen for the user calls `showOut()`, which opens and scrolls to the workout section. `wKind(w)` names what a workout is (Practice, Weekly plan, Program or Single workout) and when it is scheduled ("Wednesday, week 1 day 3", with today or tomorrow added); the sheet shows it as `.wkind`. "Equipment for this workout" (`renderPick()`, single builds only) toggles implements in the today-only tweak (`S.today.off`), so the setup itself never changes.
Continue tab layout: every card is built as an `article[data-ck]` and `contFold()` turns it into a `details.cfold` whose label is the card's eyebrow plus its h3 (cards add a hidden-in-body h3 just for the label); `S.contFold` remembers each card's open or closed choice, and in-progress cards (resume, today, goal; `CONT_OPEN`) start open. The Today's workout card (`data-ck="today"`, built as `tday` and placed right after the goal card) is always shown: today's plan and program sessions from `upcoming(1)` with Open buttons, else the workouts logged today (Done for today), else today's cached recommendation (`Store.state.rec`, button `todayrec` opens Recommended on Build), else links to get one or build. Cards are appended with `h+=`; never assign `h=` mid-render (the old Scheduled today card did and wiped the resume and plan cards). Completed programs and completed plans (card `plandone`, shown 7 days) carry `data-dismiss` and can be dismissed (✕ on the label or Dismiss in the card), stored in `Store.state.contHide`.
Workout sections: `S.warm` / `S.cool` (default on) decide whether new workouts get a warm-up and cool-down; when off, their minutes go to the main work. A skipped section on one workout moves to `w.skipped.warm` / `w.skipped.cool` and can be added back. Every section is a collapsible `details.block`; `S.fold[kind]` remembers which kinds are collapsed.
Recovery body map: `bodyMapHTML(rs)` draws an original front and back figure above the muscle list. `BODY_OUTLINE` and `BODY_PARTS` are generated from point lists (Catmull-Rom smoothed) written for one side and mirrored, with separate muscle heads (deltoids, pecs, ab segments, serratus, obliques, three quads, traps, infraspinatus, lats, erectors, glute med and max, two hamstrings, gastrocnemius and soleus, tibialis). Each region carries a `GROUPS` key and is filled by its `recoveryState()` level (paler when not worked in 10 days); the inner thigh (adductors) has no group and stays neutral. Tapping a region highlights its row in `#musc`. If a muscle group is added to `GROUPS`, give it regions here.
Muscle groups: `GROUPS` includes chest and calves. `MUSCLE_MAP` grades every library move per group (3 main, 2 worked hard, 1 supporting; kettlebell from EMG studies, mace and club estimated; reasoning and sources in project doc muscles-worked.md) and `moveMuscles(x)` returns it, falling back to `PAT_MUSCLES` + `MOVE_EXTRA` for a move without an entry: give every new move an entry. `stepMuscles(name,eq)` also resolves flow step names (`FLOW_ALIAS`, plus entries for flow-only steps such as the kb overhead hold); `flowMuscles(f)` is the highest grade per group across a flow's steps and feeds `musclesFor()` for logged flows. `mainG()` decides main movers for recovery avoidance. Logs store their muscles when saved, so older logs keep the old shares.
Demo muscles bar: demos.js keeps `ctxNow` (`{name,eq}` or `{flow}`), adds `#dmMus` to the pop-up and calls `DemoMuscles.open()` on show and `DemoMuscles.step(i,mark,label)` on every mark; `DemoMuscles` (index.html) draws a collapsible bar (`S.dmmOpen` remembers it) with a small front and back map in the equipment's colour (`dmmFig()`) and a graded list. For a flow the step playing now is in full colour and the rest of the flow faint.
Themes: `S.skin` (forge, akhara, chalk, patina) sets `data-skin` on the root and `S.appearance` (auto, light, dark) sets `data-theme`; themes only swap color tokens, including the `--eq-*` equipment colors used by the movement banners.
