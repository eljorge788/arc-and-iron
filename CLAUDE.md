# Arc & Iron

Chris's kettlebell, steel mace and steel club workout app, published with GitHub Pages at
https://eljorge788.github.io/arc-and-iron/

## Layout
- `index.html`: the whole app (markup, CSS and JS in one file, no build step).
- `sw.js`: service worker. Network-first for the page so updates reach phones on next open. Bump `CACHE` when changing icons or the manifest.
- `demos.js`: animated move demos (front and side views) for the mace, the steel club and the kettlebell. A pose's `len` picks the implement (MACE or CLUB); `kb:1` draws a kettlebell instead (G = handle middle, D = handle to bell, W = along the handle, hands at `hR`/`hL` along W and `hD` along D). Club moves follow the beginner reference in the Arc & Iron project (steel-club-exercises.md), and the side swing (two-hand and single-arm) follows steel-club-side-swing.md; kettlebell moves follow Chris's lesson notes from Mark Wildman's kettlebell lessons 1-16 and his "3 Types of Goblet Squats" (also in the project). Two clubs: a pose with `G2`/`D2` puts a second club in the left hand (on its pommel); `dblClub()` mirrors a one-hand club pose into both hands. Swings and swipes keep the arms straight on the way down: a pose with `lock` ('R', 'L', 'B' two hands, '2' two clubs) has the swinging arm straight, and between two locked poses the hand travels on an arc around the shoulder (`armArc()`); `lockSwing(kf,lock)` straightens and locks every pose of a move where the hand hangs well below the shoulder. Two kettlebells: `G2`/`D2`/`W2` put a second bell in the left hand; `dblBell()` mirrors a one-hand kettlebell pose into both hands. `carry()` moves a hold written for standing tall onto a squatting or leaning body. A pose's `twist` turns the chest about the spine (after pitch, yaw and roll; the windmill uses it to stack the shoulders). Mace holds follow Chris's definitions (project doc mace-holds.md): `STAND` is the order position (lead hand on top, hands at the belly button, mace straight up), the offset hold puts the lead hand along the shaft, choking up moves the hands toward the head, the row position (`ROW_L()`/`ROW_R()`) is a split hold with the mace hanging level at the lead side on long straight arms (a dead hang), head behind (the uppercut and paddle start there, and `swordSwitch(from)` hands it from one side's row position to the other's), and high ready (seatbelt, `HIGH_READY()`, demo `mace:high ready`) holds the mace on a diagonal across the body, lead hand at the head by the lead shoulder, base at the other hip. One-hand kettlebell positions (rack, lockout, swing and clean) are built for the right hand; `mirror()` gives the left.
- Flow demos: `Demos.openFlow()` chains the step demos. A flow listed in `FLOW_TL` (demos.js) is instead built as one continuous movement by `flowTL()`: moves run into each other with no stop at order, the cycle repeats mirrored for the other lead side, and each mark carries its step index and side for highlighting. Kettlebell flows use `flowTLs()` (parts carry their own side, or none for two-hand flows such as the Halo Flow) and `kbSidedFlow()` (right-hand parts, then mirrored for the left, with a floor-to-hike intro and a park-the-bell outro); the hand change is a hand-to-hand swing switch. Mace flows use `maceFlow(parts,names,first,steps2)`: parts are written for one lead (top) hand and mirrored; a switch step (or a part flagged true, such as the high grab into the row position) changes the lead so the labels follow it; `prayerOrder()` is the prayer switch between moves (order to a horizontal hold with the lead hand at the head, prayer at the center, horizontal on the other side, then on into the next move), `highGrabToRow()` the support hand's high overhand grab into the row position, `uppercutRep()`/`uppercutToFront()` one uppercut back to the row or down in front into order.
- `art.js`: theme-aware SVG artwork (tab backgrounds, program and skill-path heroes). Brief in `art.brief.md`. `S.art` picks poster, engraved or off.
- `keepawake.js`: keeps the screen on during guided workouts (Wake Lock API, with the NoSleep.js video fallback, MIT).
- `manifest.webmanifest` and `icons/`: home-screen install. `icons/badge.svg` is the master logo.

## Data
User data lives in each browser's localStorage (`ironflow`, `ironflow-logs`, `ironflow-checks`, `ironflow-state`, `ironflow-setup`, `ironflow-resume`). Keep these key names and record shapes backward compatible so updates never wipe anyone's history. When a move or flow is renamed, add the old name to `RENAMED` (old log name to new) so its history, last weight and progression carry over; logs themselves are never rewritten.
Equipment: `S.setups` holds named setups and `S.eq` always points at the active setup's equipment (older saves without setups are migrated into a "Home" setup by `linkSetups()`). `S.today` is a today-only tweak; the generator reads equipment through `effEq()`. The in-app backup file uses `app: "arc-and-iron"`.
In progress: `Store.state.week` is the saved weekly plan: `plan` is an array of `{days}` (objects, not nested arrays, so it syncs), one per week; `phases` names each week (learn, build, push, light; `S.planWeeks` 1/4/6 picks the length); `done` and `skip` map `"week:Day"` to time; each day's `weekRef` is `{id,day,wk}`. Week 1 is built up front; later days are stubs that `ensureBuilt()` builds when first opened (Build/Push weeks reuse the week-1 moves via `refreshWorkout()`, which reworks weights and targets from the latest logs; the Lighter week is a shorter fresh build with `opts.light`). `planNorm()` migrates older one-week saves (`days`, `done` keyed by day) and must be used instead of reading `Store.state.week` directly. Startup shows a saved plan and never rebuilds over it; Build plan asks before replacing one in progress. `Store.state.saved` (via `savedList()`) keeps every single workout built and not yet logged (newest first, up to 8, 21 days), so a new build never overwrites another; `current.sid` is the saved entry on screen and `keepBuilt()` replaces it in place. `Store.state.lastBuilt` mirrors the newest for older code. The Build tab shows chips for the plan and each saved workout (`buildBarHTML()`). `ironflow-resume` holds an unfinished guided workout.
Programs: `Store.state.programs` (via `progList()`) lists every running program `{id,start,done,days}`; `days` are the chosen weekdays for Day 1, Day 2 ... (`suggestDays()` picks free, spaced days; `schedPickHTML()` is the picker). Older saves with one `Store.state.program` are moved into the list. `progList()` and `savedList()` edit their arrays in place: never hold one across a call that might reassign it.
Schedule: `upcoming(n)` places every unfinished plan and program session on its next matching weekday from today. The log calendar (`renderCal()`), Continue's "Scheduled today", the program picker and extra workouts read it. Single builds and the recommendation pass `opts.around` from `schedAround()`, which avoids the main muscle groups of today's unfinished and tomorrow's sessions and follows the plan's lighter week.
Weights: `wtAdj()`/`stepWt()` let you step any move's weight through your rack before starting (`orig` keeps the built weight); the log form steps through the rack too and shows the planned weight. Hands: `HAND_SETS` groups moves that are the same exercise one-handed, two-handed or with two implements (two bells / two clubs); `handSeg()` draws the picker and `switchHands()` swaps in the real move from X (new cue, demo, history and per-side dose; a weight changed by hand carries over). The picker sits beside the weight on the workout sheet, in guided mode (current move, list workouts and the Get ready screen, with − / + via `gMoveCtl()`; `gRegen()` rebuilds the steps and keeps your place) and on the log form (`data-lh`, which also sets `dbl`). Two-implement options need matching pairs in the setup, except on the log. Guided steps carry `secI/secT` (working sets in the section) and `mvI/mvT` (per move) for the sets done/left line (`gSetsHTML()`). Logging a workout calls `finishedWorkout()` to tick these off. The Continue tab reads all of them.
Goal and achievements: `Store.state.goal` is the weekly goal (`hist` keeps the goal in force each week, so changing it never rewrites past weeks). `Store.state.ach` maps achievement id to the time earned and is never recomputed away; the first run credits past history silently. `Store.state.plansDone` lists weekly plans finished in full. Achievements are defined in `ACH` (groups in `ACH_GROUPS`); records come from `achCtx()` / `recordsIn()`. Log records may carry `kinds` (workout styles), `counts` (guided AMRAP rounds, ladder rungs, deck cards, with `kind` and `min`), `guided` and `restFlow`; older logs simply lack them.
Coach (automatic progression, on unless `S.prog===false`): `progPick()` starts each move from its last logged weight and steps up one rack weight after two good sessions (per-move `feel` e/r/h on log moves, else session effort ≤7), holds on Hard, and drops one step in a lighter week (`Store.state.coach.deload` = that Monday). `coachBlock()` sets AMRAP/deck targets, EMOM reps (log field `emom`) and ladder height (count field `top`). Moves/flows that changed carry `up:{from,dir}`; `undoProg()` reverts a workout and sets `opts.noProg`. `coachChecks()` (on every renderTrack) starts lighter weeks, changes `S.level` (never within 21 days of `S.levelAt`) and marks skill steps, each leaving a note in `Store.state.coach.notes` with Undo. When a move is ready to go up but the rack has nothing heavier, `progPick()` returns `need:{next,cur,unit,dbl}` (next standard size from `EQ[eq].lists`); `gearHTML()` suggests it with an add-to-setup button. Guided mode inserts a `prep` step ("Get ready", `S.prep` seconds, default 30, 0 = off; cool-down capped at 10) before every section after the first, never inside one. The recommended workout is `recommend()`, cached per day in `Store.state.rec` by `recSig()`.
Skill paths: progress is `Store.state.skills` keyed `eq:stepIndex`. The kettlebell path was reordered to the lesson order (ten steps); `migrateKbPath()` (run from `KS()`) moves older saves onto the new steps once and sets `Store.state.kbPath=2`. If a path's steps are ever reordered again, add a migration like it rather than shifting indexes silently.
Lead side: every club and mace move (and every one-sided move, flag `u`) has a lead side; `sided()` decides. The lead side is the top hand, for both club and mace; a two-hand club is held on the top hand's side of the body (in demos, `topSwap()` fixes a move built with the club on the other side). On one-handed moves it is the working hand. Order positions favour the lead side (two-hand `C_ORDER2` sits toward the top hand; one-hand `C_ORDER1` is at the working shoulder). Workouts call out the switch: halfway through each interval (guided mode says "Switch lead side" at the halfway mark via `step.half`), reps split per side in EMOM, AMRAP, ladder, deck and strength, each time the move comes round in Tabata, and halfway through club and mace warm-up drills. Every club and mace flow has a hand switch step (or ends on a switch move, such as the switch shield cast, that hands the club over); `sw:'mid'` flows cover both lead sides in one cycle, `sw:'end'` flows switch at the end so cycles alternate. Keep `u` for one-handed moves; don't mark two-hand club or mace moves `u` to get side handling. Switch moves (flag `a`, hands change every rep) have the switch built in: `sided()` is false for them and their doses say "alternating hands".
Hands: club moves are two-handed unless their flags say `1` (one hand) or `2` (two clubs); `handsOf()` gives the label ("One hand", "Two hands", "Two clubs") shown on workout cards, guided lists and guided steps. A club move that can be done either way is two separate entries (e.g. Torch press and Two-hand torch press) so a workout always names the version; the demo pop-up can still switch between them via `alt`.
Workout sections: `S.warm` / `S.cool` (default on) decide whether new workouts get a warm-up and cool-down; when off, their minutes go to the main work. A skipped section on one workout moves to `w.skipped.warm` / `w.skipped.cool` and can be added back. Every section is a collapsible `details.block`; `S.fold[kind]` remembers which kinds are collapsed.
Themes: `S.skin` (forge, akhara, chalk, patina) sets `data-skin` on the root and `S.appearance` (auto, light, dark) sets `data-theme`; themes only swap color tokens, including the `--eq-*` equipment colors used by the movement banners.

## Working on it
- Test changes before pushing (a jsdom smoke test is enough: build a workout, run guided mode, log it, check Programs).
- Pushing to `main` publishes the site within a couple of minutes.
- Keep the copy plain and the training cues safety-minded; it is a training aid, not medical advice.
