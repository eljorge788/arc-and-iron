# Arc & Iron

Kettlebell, steel mace and steel club workouts built from the gear you own.

- Builds single workouts or weekly plans from your weights (lb or kg) and the time you have
- Guided workout mode with timers, demos, and "last time" weights
- Flows, intervals, EMOM, Tabata, AMRAP, ladders and deck-of-cards conditioning
- Multi-week programs and step-by-step skill paths for mace, club and kettlebell
- Workout log, progress charts and a muscle recovery estimate
- Installable on your phone's home screen and works offline

All data stays on your device. Use **Back up your data** in the app to move it to another phone.

This is a training aid, not medical advice. Train in clear space and stop a set when form fades.
